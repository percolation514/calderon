The name

Every line has to earn its place, in a sonnet or in a website.

Calderon takes its name from Pedro Calderón de la Barca, the Spanish Golden Age playwright whose plays were built with the same care he gave every line of verse in them. That kind of craftsmanship, with nothing loose and nothing left to chance, is what we aim for in every site we design.
